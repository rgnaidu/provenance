Limitations
 - Heavy crops may break soft binding. 
 - Extreme screenshots may reduce ORB matches. 
 - Large content edits can evade soft matching. 
 - Some dataset assets do not include manifests. 
 - Revoked issuers are treated as untrusted provenance. 
 - Absence of provenance is not evidence of fabrication. 
 - Verified provenance does not prove that the scene is true or not staged.
